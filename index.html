I link the IT department and Earth Sciences at the University of Iceland. Much of my work is helping researchers process geospatial data of any kind on the university HPC systems, and building the tools that make that easier. I’m also doing a second master’s, in Geophysics.

Now

Esri on Linux HPC

Setting up ArcGIS Enterprise (ArcGIS Server, Image Server and Notebook Server) to process and analyse geospatial data.

Research data platform

Building a map-based platform that helps Earth scientists in Iceland find and preview research data.

Earlier

Geospatial equipment and software

Three years in geomatics: preparing, testing and configuring equipment, training users and giving technical support. Drones, GNSS receivers, total stations, mobile and static lidar scanners, 360° lidar cameras, ground penetrating radar and more.

Cadaster automation with ArcPy

ArcMap toolboxes for Romanian land registration, developed for real data from three rural communes in Gorj County.
